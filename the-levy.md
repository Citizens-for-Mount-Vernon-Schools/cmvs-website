## The basics

On October 21, 2026, the Mount Vernon School Board voted to place a **capital levy** before voters on the **February 9, 2027** ballot. The levy would fund improvements to our outdoor athletic facilities at Mount Vernon High School.

## What it builds

- **Covered stadium seating with ADA access.** Mount Vernon High School is one of the largest high schools in the region without covered stadium seating.
- **New restrooms, concessions and storage.**
- **A lighted multipurpose turf field** for more sports, practices and community use.
- **Upgraded stadium lights.**

## What it costs

The levy is estimated at **28 cents per $1,000 of assessed property value** for six years. On a $500,000 home, that's about **$140 a year**, or less than $12 a month.

## How the plan was made

More than 25 parents, coaches, faculty and community leaders served on the district's Outdoor Athletic Advisory Committee. They spent months studying our facilities, compared them with similar schools across the region, and reached a unanimous recommendation.

The district then asked the community. In a fall 2026 survey, 619 people responded, and **77% favored** the stadium and field improvements in this levy.

## Why it matters

- **Safety and access.** Safe, welcoming, accessible spaces for students, families and fans.
- **School and community pride.** Our stadium hosts games, graduation and community gatherings.
- **Long-term value.** Facilities that serve the next generation of Mount Vernon students.
