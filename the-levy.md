## Approved by the School Board

The Mount Vernon School District's Outdoor Athletic Advisory Committee studied our facilities and developed options for improving them, and the district asked the community for feedback. After reviewing those options and the community's input, the **Mount Vernon School District Board of Directors approved Resolution No. 01/26-27 on October 21, 2026**. It places a six-year capital levy before voters as **Proposition 1, Capital Levy to Improve School Facilities**, at a special election on **February 9, 2027**.

The Board found that improvements are needed to make students, staff and the community safer, increase opportunities for student athletics and activities, improve accessibility for all users, and increase community use of district facilities.

## What it builds

All improvements are at Mount Vernon High School:

- **Covered bleachers with ADA-accessible seating** for athletics, graduation and other events. Mount Vernon High School is one of the largest high schools in the region without covered stadium seating.
- **A permanent public restroom, concessions facility, storage area and ticket booth** at the stadium.
- **An all-weather turf multipurpose field with field lighting** for baseball, softball, soccer, band and community use.
- **Upgraded stadium lights** and other safety, accessibility and athletic facility improvements.

## What it costs

The estimated rate is **28 cents per $1,000 of assessed property value** each year for six years. On a $500,000 home, that's about **$140 a year**, or less than $12 a month.

By law, the money from this levy can only be used for the construction, modernization or remodeling of school facilities. It can't be used for salaries or classroom programs.

## How the plan was made

More than 25 parents, coaches, faculty and community leaders served on the Outdoor Athletic Advisory Committee. They spent months studying our facilities, compared them with similar schools across the region, and reached a unanimous recommendation.

The district then asked the community. In a fall 2026 survey, 619 people responded, and **77% favored** the stadium and field improvements now included in this levy.

## Why it matters

- **Safety and access.** Safe, welcoming, accessible spaces for students, families and fans.
- **More opportunities for students.** More room for baseball, softball, soccer, band and other activities.
- **School and community pride.** Our stadium hosts games, graduation and community gatherings.
- **Long-term value.** Facilities that serve the next generation of Mount Vernon students.
