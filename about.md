## Our mission

Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools is a group of community members who support our public schools. We share clear, factual information and encourage everyone to get involved.

## Who we are

We are volunteers: parents, grandparents, alumni, retirees and local business owners. Anyone who cares about Mount Vernon's students is welcome to join us.
