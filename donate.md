## Where your money goes

Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools is a volunteer campaign committee. Every dollar goes to running the campaign: yard signs, mailers, printing, advertising and outreach, so every voter in Mount Vernon hears about the levy before February 9.

**Your donation goes to the campaign committee, not to the Mount Vernon School District.** The levy itself is paid for by property taxes if voters approve it. Contributions to a political committee are not tax-deductible.

## Give by check

Make your check payable to **Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools** and mail it to:

**Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools**<br>
PO Box 2944<br>
Mount Vernon, WA 98273

Please include your name, address, occupation and employer. Washington's Public Disclosure Commission requires campaigns to report this information for contributions over $100.
