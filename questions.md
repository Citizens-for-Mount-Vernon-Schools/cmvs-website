intro: Straight answers to the questions we hear most.
questions:
  - question: When is the election?
    answer: The levy is on the **February 9, 2027** ballot. Washington votes by mail, so your ballot arrives at home a few weeks before. Return it by mail (no stamp needed) or in a county drop box by 8 p.m. on February 9.
  - question: How much will it cost me?
    answer: An estimated **28 cents per $1,000 of assessed value** for six years. On a $500,000 home, that's about $140 a year, or less than $12 a month. To estimate yours, divide your home's assessed value by 1,000 and multiply by $0.28.
  - question: Why spend on athletics when the district has cut staff and programs?
    answer: It's a fair question. A capital levy can only be spent on buildings and facilities. It can't be used to pay for teachers, staff or classroom programs, and voting no would not move this money into the classroom.
  - question: What exactly would be built?
    answer: Covered stadium seating with ADA access, new restrooms, concessions and storage, a lighted multipurpose turf field, and upgraded stadium lights. See [The Levy](../the-levy/) for details.
  - question: Who decided what's in the levy?
    answer: More than 25 parents, coaches, faculty and community leaders on the district's Outdoor Athletic Advisory Committee studied our facilities for months, compared them with similar schools across the region, and reached a unanimous recommendation. A community survey followed, and 77% of 619 respondents favored these improvements.
  - question: Will the community be able to use the new facilities?
    answer: Our stadium already hosts graduation, games and community events, and a multipurpose field adds more room for school and community use. Ask us if you have specific questions about scheduling or access.
  - question: Who is Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools?
    answer: We're a volunteer group of Mount Vernon parents, grandparents, alumni, coaches, business owners and neighbors. We are not the school district. We formed to help pass this levy.
  - question: How do I register to vote or check my registration?
    answer: Go to [VoteWA.gov](https://voter.votewa.gov). You can register online or update your address there.
