## Approved by the Mount Vernon School Board

After reviewing the options developed by the district's Outdoor Athletic Advisory Committee and feedback from the community, the Mount Vernon School District Board of Directors approved **Resolution No. 01/26-27** on October 21, 2026. It places this capital levy before voters as **Proposition 1** on the February 9, 2027 ballot.

## Why it matters

Our students give their best every day, on the field and in the classroom. More than 25 parents, coaches, faculty and community leaders spent months studying our outdoor athletic facilities. They compared them with similar schools across our region and reached a **unanimous recommendation**. In a district survey of 619 community members, **77% supported** this set of improvements.

Now the plan goes to the voters, and good ideas don't pass on their own. Levies are passed by neighbors talking to neighbors.
