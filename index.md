## Why we're here

Our schools are the heart of Mount Vernon. Citizens for Mount Vernon Schools brings people together to learn about the issues facing our district, share accurate information, and support our students, teachers and staff.

## How you can help

- **Come to an event.** Meet your neighbors and hear what's happening.
- **Spread the word.** Share our updates with friends and family.
- **Volunteer.** Even an hour makes a difference.
